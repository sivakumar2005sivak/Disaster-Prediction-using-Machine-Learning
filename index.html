Disaster Preparedness & Public Safety Guidelines

Official NDMA / SDMA Standards

Immediate action protocols, essential evacuation checklists, and emergency response contacts for citizens.

🚨 Unified Helpline: 112
🌊

Flood & Inundation Safety

  • Turn Off Utilities: Shut off the main electrical breaker switch and gas cylinders immediately before vacating.
  • Never Drive or Wade in Water: As little as 6 inches of moving floodwater can sweep a person, and 12 inches can float a car.
  • Move to Higher Ground: Evacuate to designated multi-hazard shelters or upper concrete floors.
  • Safe Drinking Water: Drink only boiled or chlorinated water to prevent waterborne illnesses (cholera, leptospirosis).
⛰️

Landslide & Slope Hazards

  • Early Warning Sounds: Listen for unusual sounds like trees cracking, rumbling boulders, or sudden muddy water from drainage.
  • Inspect Structural Shifts: Look out for widening cracks on building floors, retaining walls, or leaning utility poles.
  • Evacuate Hill Slopes: Vacate cliff-edge and slope-base houses immediately during prolonged rainfall >100mm.
  • Move Perpendicular: If sudden debris flow starts, run sideways away from the channel path, never downstream.
🌪️

Cyclone & High Wind Squalls

  • Secure Loose Fixtures: Secure tin roofs, solar arrays, water tanks, and remove exterior loose objects before winds intensify.
  • Stay Clear of Glass: Keep all doors/windows firmly bolted; stay in the strongest interior room away from exterior glass.
  • Beware the Eye: If wind suddenly dies down, the storm center is passing—do not venture outside as destructive winds will resume in reverse.
  • Electrical Hazards: Steer completely clear of snapped overhead wires and submerged transformers.
☀️

Drought & Severe Heat Stress

  • Continuous Hydration: Drink adequate water, oral rehydration solutions (ORS), and traditional electrolytes (lemon water, buttermilk).
  • Avoid Peak Hours: Restrict heavy outdoor exertion and field work between 11:00 AM and 3:30 PM.
  • Conserve Water: Protect community rainwater harvesting tanks and prioritize domestic drinking supplies.
  • Livestock Shelter: Keep domestic livestock and cattle shaded with constant access to fresh water.
